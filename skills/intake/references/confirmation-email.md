# Confirmation email

A draft the recruiter edits and sends. It confirms what the client said and asks for what is missing, in the profile's locale and under 200 words where the open questions allow.

Parts, in order:

1. **Subject:** `<role_title>: spec to confirm`, with the version when it is above 1.
2. **Greeting and one line of thanks.** Name a day, date or time only if the intake states it. A date such as the call date is not a weekday, so write "Thanks for your time" and leave the day out.
3. **What we heard:** must-haves, nice-to-haves, deal-breakers, location, and any compensation or terms that were stated, in plain sentences. Inferred items are marked "please confirm".
4. **What changed:** update mode only, one line per change the client made. Your rubric and metadata changes go under "For your review".
5. **Questions:** the `open_questions`, numbered, each neutral and about what the role needs.
6. **Sign-off:** ask the client to reply with corrections, then the recruiter's name.

The body covers only these parts. Refused requests are for the recruiter to raise in their own way and words, so they appear in the chat reply under "For your review". Legal advice and statements about the law are for the recruiter and their counsel.
